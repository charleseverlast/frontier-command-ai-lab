# Frontier Command — internal campaign outline (spoilers)
## I — Echoes of the Unknown
A signal near Nacre Reach leads to a disabled transport. Rescue or scientific analysis reveals a relay. The relay reveals a fragment of a star chart. These are the implemented opening events. Further investigations should reveal the first alien contact. Routine station operations remain available.
## II — Connected Revelations
Proposed: Aethari Concord (defensive, humanitarian), Varkh Dominion (uncompromising imperial expansion), Nythari Ascendancy (pragmatic technological survival). Their competing investigations reveal a common ancient origin. Independent diplomatic and scientific routes lead to the same central mystery.
## III — Tobran Agenda
Selina Sharina Tobran has secretly directed Stratos resources toward finding the Xalarian crystal. Proposed motivation: she believes absolute energy abundance can prevent future suffering, but her desire for control increasingly conflicts with that ideal. Her deeper personal history remains open for approval. Avalar's growth toward captaincy is shaped by the Admiral's mentorship.
## IV — Gathering Storm
Competing powers demand access to discoveries. Alliances, refusals, coercion, and military actions have persistent consequences. Officers may disagree, resign or defect. Side incidents continue independently of the main plot.
## V — Xalarian Legacy
The lost civilization once harnessed the crystal safely. Proposed explanation: distributed safeguards prevented individual control; their collapse followed an attempt to centralize authority. The destruction mechanism and exact antagonist leadership remain open. Evidence is revealed through exploration and consultation, not arbitrary dialogue invention.
## VI — Final Reckoning
1. Greater Good: destroy the crystal; attempt to save Selina, who intends to perish with it. Stratos succession passes to the Admiral.
2. Burden of Power: Selina takes control; the Admiral may join, escape, or be imprisoned.
3. Rise of the Tyrant: Admiral takes control, wages war, and demands submission; officers react according to convictions and loyalty.
No morality score permanently locks an ending. Reputation unlocks influence and affects feasibility. All later acts are design only, not implemented.
